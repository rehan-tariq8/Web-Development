Microsoft Teams
Communicate with your team and work together seamlessly. The platform offers instant messaging, audio and video calling, screen sharing, file sharing, and more. With Microsoft Teams, you can work together from anywhere, on any device, and any time.
Microsoft Outlook
Microsoft Outlook is more than emails. Manage your calendar and tasks, track deadlines, and more across your desktop app, mobile app, and web browser. Integrate with Microsoft Teams and Dynamics 365 for a unified experience across Microsoft apps.
Microsoft OneDrive & SharePoint
With Microsoft OneDrive, store, share, and collaborate on files anywhere you go with Microsoft OneDrive. Manage documents, team sites, and intranet portal with Microsoft SharePoint. Combine the two for a seamless cloud-based file storage experience.
